Advantage Testing of Portland has delivered outstanding educational services throughout the Pacific Northwest since 2011. Located in Lake Oswego, Advantage Testing of Portland helps students achieve their academic goals. Working in concert with educators, counselors, and learning specialists, we develop individually tailored programs that are designed to complement a student's schoolwork and achieve a consistent strategy for success. We offer both test preparation and academic tutoring, including the development of custom curricula for students who seek academic enrichment beyond what their schools may offer.
