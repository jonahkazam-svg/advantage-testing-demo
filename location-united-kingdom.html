Advantage Testing of the United Kingdom delivers outstanding educational services, carrying on Advantage Testing's tradition of excellence in the U.K. Launched by Matthew Demmer and John Roberts, who have almost 40 years of combined experience tutoring and counseling students for admissions to top U.S. universities, Advantage Testing of the United Kingdom has a growing number of exceptional tutors with expertise in U.S. standardized entrance exams.

We develop rigorous, individually tailored programs that are designed to complement a student's schoolwork and help them achieve their academic and professional goals. Our tutors specialize in U.S. college entrance exams (ACT, SAT, and AP Exams) and U.S. college application counseling, and we offer a range of other test preparation and academic tutoring options.

Throughout its more than 35 years of operation, Advantage Testing has been a pioneer in the field of distance learning and remote tutoring. Advantage Testing of the U.K. has carried on that tradition, offering our services via a variety of remote platforms and using highly effective collaboration tools such as online whiteboard programs. We will resume in-person tutoring in students' London homes when it is safe to do so; until then, we encourage our clients to pursue online tutoring, which our organization has offered for many years and which we have found to be remarkably effective.
