Remote Learning Success

Setup guidelines

Students and teachers worldwide are adjusting to online education. The following guidelines are beneficial for tutoring and for any online learning experience.

Smartphones aren't ideal for tutoring sessions that combine video and online whiteboard features. They can receive distracting notifications. We recommend a computer or tablet, though Advantage Testing accommodates a variety of technical setups.

Built-in computer speakers and microphones have limitations. Headphones enable clearer hearing and prevent room noise from interfering. An external microphone improves a tutor's ability to hear the student.

Video conferencing drains batteries quickly. Devices should be plugged in or fully charged before sessions. A strong, reliable Wi-Fi network is essential.

Disconnecting temporarily improves session focus, as notifications are distracting. We recommend:

  • Apple: Enable "Do Not Disturb"
  • Android: Activate "Do Not Disturb"
  • Windows: Manage Quiet Hours in settings

Close unnecessary browser tabs except those needed for video conferencing, the whiteboard, and materials.

Students need a quiet, well-lit, and ideally private space. High-traffic areas like kitchens create distractions. Setup should occur before sessions begin, with household members instructed not to interrupt.

Remotely Proctored Practice Tests

Real conditions, at home

A key element of all our test preparation programs, whether in person or remote, is taking practice tests in a setting that simulates the official testing room as closely as possible. Our trained proctors administer tests in a simulated official testing environment, whether students participate in person or remotely.

Ten-Step Preparation Guide

  • Contact Advantage Testing to schedule your remotely proctored practice test.
  • Download and print the answer sheet, booklet, and essay sheets (if applicable) for your exam.
  • Obtain a paper copy or online access to your test, but refrain from starting until the proctored session begins.
  • Set up a quiet, distraction-free room for taking your test.
  • Charge and prepare your calculator if your test permits one (such as the ACT or SAT).
  • Use a desktop, laptop, or tablet equipped with camera and microphone capabilities.
  • Mute your microphone once testing starts; keep your volume on for proctor instructions; and close unnecessary browser tabs.
  • Follow proctor guidance on section transitions and breaks; you may mute both microphone and camera during breaks.
  • Have an independent stopwatch or timer available (though the proctor will direct section changes).
  • Good luck!

Key Technical Requirements

  • Desktop, laptop, or tablet device
  • Camera and microphone capability
  • Calculator (for applicable tests like the ACT or SAT)
  • Quiet, distraction-free environment
  • Paper copy or online access to the test
