Since 2008, Advantage Testing of Los Angeles has served the Los Angeles and the greater Southern California scholastic communities. Through our outstanding educational services, thousands of students have accomplished their academic and professional goals.

We develop customized tutoring programs for students ages six to 60, offering test preparation for middle school, high school, college, and graduate school admissions exams, as well as academic tutoring across a wide range of subjects.

Advantage Testing of Los Angeles maintains tutoring offices in Century City, Santa Monica, Sherman Oaks, Torrance, and Pasadena, providing our students with accessible and excellent learning environments. We also offer in-home and distance-learning options.

We maintain an active financial assistance program and partner with local nonprofits serving underserved communities.
