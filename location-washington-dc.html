Advantage Testing of Washington, DC, has served the Washington, DC, area for more than 20 years, helping thousands of students achieve their academic and professional goals. With undergraduate and graduate degrees from the nation's top colleges and universities, our tutors specialize in tailoring educational programs to each student's specific needs.

In addition to our exceptional standardized test preparation programs, our tutors offer academic tutoring to students at all educational levels from grade school to graduate school (including business and law school) and individualized application counseling services.

Located in Friendship Heights, our office is easily accessible to students throughout the metropolitan region. Distance learning is also available for those located outside the area.

Advantage Testing of Washington, DC, is dedicated to helping students of every socioeconomic background. We work with the AnBryce Foundation, a philanthropic organization that promotes social change by building a community of global citizens through education, career, cultural, and leadership opportunities to under-resourced young people.
