For more than a 15 years, Advantage Testing of South Florida has helped thousands of students achieve their academic and professional goals. Launched by Darin Kaplan in 2006, we deliver exceptional educational services throughout Southern Florida.

Advantage Testing of South Florida maintains a tutoring and administrative office in Boca Raton that provides our students with an outstanding learning environment. We also have tutors who travel to students' homes from Miami to Palm Beach. Students in other locations should speak with a program consultant to discuss tutoring options.

We offer individualized tutoring for the ACT, SAT, AP Exams, SSAT, ISEE, LSAT, GRE, GMAT, and academic subjects. Working in concert with educators, counselors, and learning specialists, we develop tailored programs that complement each student's schoolwork.

In addition to our one-on-one tutoring, we teach ACT and SAT workshops for students enrolled in Pine Crest High School, St. Andrew's School, and others.
