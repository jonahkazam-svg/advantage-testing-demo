Personal statement and application review for college, graduate school, and professional school. Across six specialized programs, we help students articulate their distinctive qualities — and write essays that express themselves with confidence, character, and creativity.

How we approach it

The method

Our approach emphasizes helping students articulate their distinctive qualities through careful essay composition. The essay development process includes brainstorming and outlining the essay, and working to achieve clarity and develop a narrative voice.

Tutors help students understand which skills and experiences are most relevant to their application, with particular attention to law, business, and medical school programs that have distinct emphases, priorities, and criteria. The organization aims for lasting educational impact: through this work, the student enhances their general writing skills and gains an appreciation for the craft of writing.

Six counseling programs

Choose a track

The application essay often represents the greatest opportunity in the admissions process for a candidate to express their unique assets. Where permitted, Advantage Testing offers a comprehensive application writing program that enables students to express themselves with confidence, character, and creativity.

Essay Development

The tutor works closely with the student to identify fresh ways of conveying their message using personal and original language true to their own voices. The essay review program consists of the following:

  • Brainstorming and outlining the essay
  • Presentation of overarching principles
  • Determining a theme
  • Building sound structure
  • Achieving clarity
  • Developing a narrative voice
  • Thorough review and commentary of the multiple drafts

Individualized Focus

We help the student understand which skills and experiences are most relevant to their application. In particular, law, business, and medical school programs have distinct emphases, priorities, and criteria.

Lasting Results

All our work aims to cultivate useful, enduring skills in the student. By learning to write carefully constructed essays, the student enhances their general writing skills and gains an appreciation for the craft of writing. The application essay is a particularly effective means of instilling writing lessons because the student is so deeply invested in the impact of their prose.

Advantage Testing's College Counseling program provides a comprehensive individual review of the most critical components of the admissions process:

  • School research and selection (considering objective and subjective criteria)
  • Academic and standardized testing plans
  • Extracurricular advising
  • Development of a central theme for the college application

The counselor works with the student to assess the student's academic, extracurricular, and personal achievements, explore areas of interest, and identify the key factors to emphasize in the application materials. These discussions frame the overarching narrative that will unify the student's college application.

In addition, counselors assist in determining the best schools for the student through detailed discussion and individually tailored research. Finally, counselors can oversee the student's application development, either working directly with the student or in conjunction with an application essay specialist.

The Law School Admissions Counseling Program entails extensive individual review of every aspect of the application cycle. Program content is tailored to each student's specific needs and focuses on the key components of the application.

Program Components

  • An initial strategic planning session and overview of services
  • Development of a holistic application strategy
  • Development of an application timeline
  • Law school selection
  • Résumé planning and review
  • Personal statement planning and review
  • Recommendation letter advice
  • Interview preparation
  • Addendum strategy
  • Application review

The program provides customized counseling throughout the application process, focusing on:

  • Premedical academic planning
  • Premedical academic tutoring (as needed)
  • Premedical extracurricular planning, especially with regard to research and volunteer work
  • Résumé preparation and review
  • Development of an application timeline
  • Review of premedical essays required by most undergraduate programs
  • Recommendation letter advice
  • AMCAS personal statement planning and review
  • Secondary applications planning and feedback
  • Interview preparation, both for traditional interviews and those in the new Multiple Mini Interview (MMI) format

Advantage Testing offers a distinctive interview training program designed to improve an applicant's interviewing and presentation skills. Students receive training and practice explicitly adapted to the type of interview they are preparing to face. By rehearsing the interview process with one of our expert counselors, candidates can gain confidence in their abilities to communicate their strengths effectively and express themselves clearly. Applicants who complete our interview training program are better equipped for future oral presentations and personal interviews in a range of contexts.

Program Components

The training program includes the following components:

  • Discussion of interviewing techniques, encompassing verbal and nonverbal communication
  • Mock interview
  • In-depth review of the mock interview in its entirety, including such dimensions as the interviewee's organization, focus, responsiveness, tone, pacing, and nonverbal communication
  • Recommendations for improving the substance and presentation of the candidate's responses to an interviewer's questions
